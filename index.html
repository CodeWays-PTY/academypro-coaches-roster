Purpose-Built Tools for Modern Sports Coaching

We designed and built two dedicated platforms to simplify the daily workflows of athletic directors, head coaches, and strength specialists.

smartphone Coach Mobile App (Flutter)

Field-Side Speed, Strength & Check-In Logging

Built with Flutter for high performance on both iOS and Android devices, the Coach Mobile App allows coaches to record physical test scores, athlete body metrics, and squad check-ins directly on the field.

  • ✓
    Rapid Attendance Check-ins: Log session attendance in seconds with quick toggle states and safe touch target interfaces.
  • ✓
    Field Metric Entry: Instantly record speed splits, 1RM strength scores, conditioning times, and body weight measurements.
  • ✓
    Seamless Remote Sync: Data captured on field mobile devices syncs directly to the central cloud database for real-time coach visibility.
laptop Coach Web Portal

Centralized Squad Roster & Performance Analytics

The Coach Web Portal gives head coaches and athletic directors an administrative command center to manage squad rosters, set performance metric baselines, and oversee targeted Action Plans.

  • ✓
    Roster Administration: Manage athlete profiles, squad allocations, playing positions, and physical measurement history in one central hub.
  • ✓
    Baseline Performance Analytics: Track historical test scores, identify athletic benchmarks, and measure squad development over time.
  • ✓
    Targeted Action Plans: Create custom developmental targets for individual athletes with automated 24-hour completion workflows.
